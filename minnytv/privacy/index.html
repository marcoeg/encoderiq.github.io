Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the handling of information by MinnyTV YouTube Publisher, an internal application used by MinnyTV to publish MinnyTV-produced content to the MinnyTV YouTube channel.

YouTube API Services

MinnyTV YouTube Publisher uses the YouTube Data API and Google OAuth 2.0. By using YouTube API Services, the application is subject to the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and applicable Google and YouTube policies.

YouTube Terms of Service: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Information accessed

The application is an internal first-party publishing tool and does not provide services to third-party users. The application requests Google OAuth authorization solely to access the MinnyTV YouTube channel for authorized publishing operations.

The current OAuth authorization is limited to permissions required to upload videos to YouTube.

Use of information

Information obtained through Google or YouTube APIs is used only for operating MinnyTV's internal YouTube publishing workflow, including uploading MinnyTV-produced videos and associated metadata.

MinnyTV does not sell Google or YouTube account information or API data and does not use such information for advertising.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials required for the internal publisher are stored securely and are accessible only to authorized MinnyTV personnel and systems. Credentials are not embedded in publicly accessible website content or source code.

Data retention and deletion

OAuth authorization information is retained only while required to operate the MinnyTV publishing workflow. Stored authorization credentials can be deleted when access is no longer required.

Access can also be revoked at any time through the Google Account permissions page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

Following revocation or termination of the integration, locally stored OAuth authorization credentials associated with the application can be deleted from MinnyTV systems.

Third parties

MinnyTV YouTube Publisher is not offered to third-party users and does not provide Google or YouTube account information to third-party customers.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality or data practices of MinnyTV YouTube Publisher change.
